How a project runs

  1. We talk. You tell me what the piece needs, and I scope the work before it starts.
  2. I sketch. Early mockups against picture or a rough demo, so we agree on direction before the detail.
  3. We revise. I work from your notes, and re-adapt the music when the cut changes.
  4. I deliver. Clearly named, organised files, checked against your delivery specs before they go out.

Every project is scoped on its own. Tell me about yours.
